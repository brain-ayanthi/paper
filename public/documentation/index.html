What's changed since your last update

The headlines of this release, each linking to its full guide.

Store credit

Turn customer overpayments into reusable credit, then apply all or part of that balance at a future checkout.

Batch-wise pricing

Keep a separate cost, selling price and MRP for each batch, with safe fallback prices for existing stock.

Batch-aware inventory

Carry batch prices through receiving, adjustments and transfers, then review each lot in Available Stock and exports.

WhatsApp sharing

Share receipts, quotations, credit notes, statements and payment vouchers through Click-to-Chat or Meta Cloud API.

Custom payment methods

Add offline tender types such as Mobile Money and require the cashier to record the customer's transaction reference.

Shorter navigation

Scan a cleaner sidebar while keeping every permitted destination available through grouped setup menus and Ctrl+K.

Also improved in this release: accounting for overpayments, supplier advances and delayed posting, safer batch selection and stock validation, clearer customer statements, stronger product-code and number-format validation, and safer spreadsheet exports.
